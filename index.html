Help & Support

Need help with registration? Check the information below.

📝 Registration

Select your district first. Then choose your college and complete the registration form.

🏫 College

Select your college from the list. If your college is not listed, choose Other College.

🎭 Events

Choose the event you want to participate in before submitting your registration.

💻 Technical Help

If you experience a problem while registering, contact the Chiguru organizing team.

Why can't I select a college?

Select a district first. The college list will then become available.

What if my college isn't listed?

Select "Other College" and enter your college name and address.

Can I register for an event?

Select your preferred event in the registration form and submit your details.

About Chiguru

Chiguru is the annual cultural celebration of Cambridge Institute of Technology.

It brings together students to showcase their talent, creativity and passion through cultural performances, competitions and exciting activities.

Chiguru is a celebration of talent, friendship, creativity and unforgettable memories.
